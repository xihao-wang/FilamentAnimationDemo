# Fox sample model

Source: Khronos Group glTF Sample Assets, `Models/Fox/glTF-Binary/Fox.glb`.

The model has three animation clips: Survey, Walk, and Run. It is included only as a
replaceable development asset for verifying GLB loading and skeletal animation.

- Model by PixelMannen (CC0 1.0)
- Animation/conversion contributions by tomkranis (CC BY 4.0), Asobo Studio, and scurest

Full upstream credits and license details:
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox

# Warrior character model

Source: Quaternius, `LowPoly RPG Characters`, Warrior model.

- Official release: https://www.patreon.com/posts/rpg-characters-43762126
- Author-hosted mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-rpg-characters
- Original archive: `rpg_characters_-_nov_2020.zip`
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
- Original archive SHA-256: `5399e0cfaf313ff362455de4086488d093465434b1b93b0ced649f3773a15fd7`

`warrior.glb` was converted from the original `FBX/Warrior.fbx` with FBX2glTF 0.9.7.
The original `Warrior_Texture.png` and `Warrior_Sword_Texture.png` are embedded as
base-color textures. The redundant transform on the skinned body node was normalized
to conform to glTF skinning rules. No mesh, skeleton, or animation content was removed.

The GLB contains the human Warrior, its sword, one skin, and 14 animation clips,
including `CharacterArmature|Walk`, `CharacterArmature|Sword_Attack`, and
`CharacterArmature|Sword_AttackFast`.
